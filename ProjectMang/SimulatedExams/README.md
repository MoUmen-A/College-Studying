## 🎯 Exam Structure & Marking Rubric (50 Marks Each)

Each 2-hour exam is structured specifically to eliminate trivial memorization and test deep conceptual mastery:

1. **Section A: Advanced Multiple Choice Questions (10 Marks, 1 Mark each)**
   - 10 multi-part, non-trivial MCQs testing edge cases, PMI statistics, and traps across Ch1, Ch2, and Ch3.
2. **Section B: Critical Statement Analysis & Conceptual Discrimination (10 Marks, 2 Marks each)**
   - 5 statements requiring **True/False verdict PLUS mandatory theoretical justification or error correction** (0.5 mark for verdict, 1.5 marks for rationale).
3. **Section C: Technical Terminology & Core Definitions (5 Marks, 1 Mark each)**
   - 5 precise Fill-in-the-Blank definitions across the 10 Knowledge Areas and 5 Process Groups.
4. **Section D: Deep Conceptual Explanations & Essay Problems (10 Marks, 5 Marks each)**
   - 2 in-depth multi-part questions requiring clear theoretical synthesis and concrete IT examples.
5. **Section E: Analytical Comparison & Trade-Off Matrix (5 Marks)**
   - 1 comprehensive tabular comparison matrix (e.g. Functional vs Matrix vs Projectized structures, Predictive vs Adaptive, Program vs Portfolio).
6. **Section F: Real-World Applied Case Study & Problem Solving (10 Marks)**
   - An applied enterprise IT scenario testing organizational frames, governance, Triple Constraint trade-offs, and process group interventions.
