### Section F: Real-World Applied Case Study & Problem Solving (10 Marks)
*Carefully read the IT project scenario and answer the three applied diagnostic tasks that follow.*

#### Case Scenario: Hospital Electronic Health Records (EHR) Modernization Crisis

St. Jude Regional Healthcare has initiated a \$5M project to transition 1,200 clinical staff from paper records to an integrated Electronic Health Records (EHR) platform. The hospital operates as a traditional **Functional Organization**. 
Dr. Bennett, a senior medical administrator, was appointed Project Manager, but functional heads (Chief of Surgery, Pharmacy Director, IT Director) refuse to release their top clinical specialists for user testing. Furthermore, doctors are revolting against the new software, claiming it slows patient rounds and disrupts traditional hospital culture. 
The IT Director insists on deploying the software immediately without a formal project charter or baseline plan to meet an arbitrary regulatory deadline.

#### Tasks & Deliverables:
- **1. Organizational & Frame Diagnosis (3 Marks):** Identify the organizational structure impediment. Using Bolman and Deal's Four Frames, explain how the PM can resolve the physicians' revolt through the *Political* and *Symbolic* frames.
- **2. Governance & Initiating Process Group (3 Marks):** Why was proceeding without a formal Project Charter disastrous? Who must issue the charter, and what specific authorities does it confer to Dr. Bennett?
- **3. Triple Constraint Trade-Off (4 Marks):** The regulatory deadline cannot be moved (fixed Time). Analyze how the PM must adjust Scope, Cost, and Quality to avoid clinical errors while achieving compliance.
