### Section F: Real-World Applied Case Study & Problem Solving (10 Marks)
*Carefully read the IT project scenario and answer the three applied diagnostic tasks that follow.*

#### Case Scenario: Global Cloud Migration with Cross-Cultural Virtual Teams

OmniCorp is migrating its legacy mainframe systems to a multi-region cloud infrastructure. The core project team consists of 15 system architects in New York, a software development outsourcing vendor in Bangalore (10.5 hour time difference), and a quality assurance testing group in Dublin. 
Three months into the project, deliverables are failing integration tests. The New York team complains that the Bangalore team delivers code that diverges from architectural specifications, while the Bangalore team argues that New York changes requirements in midnight emails without updating documentation. Cultural misunderstandings and lack of trust have crippled progress.

#### Tasks & Deliverables:
- **1. IT Trends Analysis (3 Marks):** Identify the IT trends present in this scenario (Globalization, Outsourcing, Virtual Teams). Explain the specific operational vulnerabilities that emerged from these trends.
- **2. Organizational Culture & Integration (3 Marks):** How does 'strong unit integration' and 'high member identity' resolve the finger-pointing between global sub-teams? What tools should be deployed?
- **3. Executing & Communications Management (4 Marks):** As the PM, detail four specific managerial interventions to repair communications, align documentation, and restore accountability across these time zones.
