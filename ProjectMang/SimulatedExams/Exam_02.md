### Section F: Real-World Applied Case Study & Problem Solving (10 Marks)
*Carefully read the IT project scenario and answer the three applied diagnostic tasks that follow.*

#### Case Scenario: Fintech Mobile Wallet Sprint & Regulatory Collision

ApexFin, a high-growth financial technology firm, is developing a next-generation AI-driven mobile wallet. The startup utilizes an **Agile/Scrum** methodology with 2-week sprints. 
Halfway through execution, national banking regulators announce severe new anti-money-laundering (AML) cryptographic standards that must be implemented within 6 weeks, or the app will face a nationwide ban. 
The development team complains that adding these compliance features will derail the user interface features promised to venture capital investors. The Product Owner wants to postpone compliance, while the Lead Architect demands halting all development to rewrite backend security from scratch.

#### Tasks & Deliverables:
- **1. Methodology & Backlog Management (3 Marks):** Explain how the Product Owner and Scrum team should handle this massive change using Scrum artifacts and ceremonies (Product Backlog, Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum).
- **2. Process Group Coordination & Phase Exits (3 Marks):** Why must the firm conduct an immediate management review (Phase Exit / Kill Point)? How do Monitoring & Controlling processes interact with Planning here?
- **3. Conflict Resolution & Triple Constraint (4 Marks):** Statistics show that schedules are the #1 cause of project conflict. How should the PM resolve the collision between investor UI features and regulatory AML compliance?
