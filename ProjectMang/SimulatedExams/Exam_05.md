### Section F: Real-World Applied Case Study & Problem Solving (10 Marks)
*Carefully read the IT project scenario and answer the three applied diagnostic tasks that follow.*

#### Case Scenario: Cybersecurity Infrastructure Defense & Scope Recovery

SecuritasNet was hired by a commercial bank to install an enterprise intrusion detection and prevention system with an approved baseline budget of \$1.2M and a 6-month timeline. 
During the **Executing Process Group**, the bank's Chief Risk Officer demands the immediate inclusion of cloud data loss prevention (DLP) and automated biometric authentication modules to counter emerging ransomware threats. The bank's junior liaison verbally promised these additions to executives. 
SecuritasNet's lead technical engineer begins coding the biometric interface immediately without notifying the Project Manager. The project is now facing severe cost overruns and missed milestones.

#### Tasks & Deliverables:
- **1. Scope Creep & Integrated Change Control (4 Marks):** Define 'Scope Creep' and contrast it with 'Progressive Elaboration'. Detail the formal Integrated Change Control process that should have occurred before any biometric coding began.
- **2. Knowledge Area Authority (3 Marks):** In which Knowledge Area does the Project Manager hold primary responsibility for unifying all project activities? How does the PM assert control over the unauthorized technical work?
- **3. Baselines & Stakeholder Management (3 Marks):** Explain why baselines (Scope, Schedule, Cost) cannot be modified unilaterally. How should the PM update the Stakeholder Register and handle the bank's Chief Risk Officer?
