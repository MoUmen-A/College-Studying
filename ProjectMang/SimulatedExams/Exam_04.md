### Section F: Real-World Applied Case Study & Problem Solving (10 Marks)
*Carefully read the IT project scenario and answer the three applied diagnostic tasks that follow.*

#### Case Scenario: Enterprise ERP Implementation: Turnaround from Total Collapse

A nationwide logistics firm launched an Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) overhaul budgeted at \$8M. Six months in, costs have ballooned to \$11M, the project is 5 months behind schedule, and functional department heads (Fleet, Warehousing, HR, Finance) are in open warfare over custom software modifications. 
An external project audit reveals that the project was initiated informally by the former CIO without a business case or project charter. Staff members are assigned 'part-time' while trying to fulfill their full-time operational duties, resulting in severe burnout and conflicting priorities.

#### Tasks & Deliverables:
- **1. Process Groups & Pre-Initiation Remediation (3 Marks):** Explain why skipping pre-initiation and Initiating caused this breakdown. What two mandatory documents must be drafted immediately to re-anchor the project?
- **2. Operations vs. Projects Conflict (3 Marks):** Why does having team members balance operational jobs with project work create systemic failure? How does organizational structure play a role?
- **3. Executive Sponsorship & PMO Intervention (4 Marks):** Describe the critical role of top management commitment in this turnaround. How can establishing a Project Management Office (PMO) enforce standards and restore control?
