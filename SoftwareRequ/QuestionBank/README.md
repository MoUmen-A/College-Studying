## Recommended Study & Self-Grading Workflow

To achieve the full mark on your university examination without relying on trial-and-error:

1. **Read the Examinee Strategic Briefing First:**
   - Every exam file begins with a dedicated **🎓 Examinee Strategic Briefing: How to Solve & Critical Pitfalls to Avoid**.
   - Read this briefing *before* looking at the questions. It provides the diagnostic problem-solving framework, section-by-section breakdown rules, and the top cognitive traps students fall into.

2. **Simulate Real Exam Conditions:**
   - Open your target exam file.
   - Solve all questions in writing without consulting the answers.
   - Do NOT scroll past the `---` horizontal rule until you have written out your full answers.
   - Time yourself: allow **75–90 minutes** per 50-mark exam.

3. **Self-Grade Against the Guide Answers:**
   - Scroll to `# GUIDE ANSWERS & MARKING SCHEME`.
   - Score yourself strictly using the **Marking Scheme** rubric.
   - Read the **Common Mistake Warning** for each question to diagnose any lingering misunderstandings.

3. **Master Surgical Rewrites:**
   - Ensure every rewritten requirement uses:
     - RFC 2119 keyword **`SHALL`**.
     - A persistent, unique ID (e.g., `FR-MHC-01`, `NFR-POS-01`).
     - Zero subjective adjectives (replace "fast", "user-friendly", "appropriate" with quantified metrics).
