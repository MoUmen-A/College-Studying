## 🎯 Pedagogical Exam Architecture (50 Marks Each)

Each 2-hour exam follows an applied, non-trivial structure:

1. **Section A: Requirements Defect Analysis & Surgical Rewrite (10 Marks, 5 Marks each)**
   - Diagnose flawed, ambiguous, compound, or untestable statements from real systems.
   - Explicitly identify violated quality characteristics (from the 9 core characteristics) and perform surgical rewrites into atomic, verifiable specifications using standard RFC 2119 keywords (`SHALL`).
2. **Section B: Conceptual Discrimination & Theoretical Rationale (10 Marks, 5 Marks each)**
   - In-depth theoretical prompts testing the *why* and *how* behind core principles (e.g., Validation vs. Verification, Standish Group 12.3% omissions failure analysis, Business Rules as enterprise assets, NFR architectural impact).
3. **Section C: Elicitation Technique Strategy & Trade-Off Analysis (10 Marks, 5 Marks each)**
   - Real-world organizational scenarios requiring the student to compare and select the optimal elicitation technique (Observation, Facilitated Workshops, Prototyping, Surveys, Focus Groups, Document Analysis) and establish concrete risk mitigations (such as overcoming the Hawthorne Effect or workshop stalemates).
4. **Section D: Artifact Engineering & Scope Modeling (10 Marks, 5 Marks each)**
   - Hands-on construction of industry artifacts: 6-clause Vision Statements, Context Diagrams, Ecosystem Maps, 3-level Feature Trees, classified Event Lists, Data Dictionaries, and Decision Trees.
5. **Section E: Comprehensive Real-World Case Study Engineering (10 Marks)**
   - An end-to-end multi-disciplinary case study requiring system boundary definition, measurable Non-Functional Requirements authoring, interface analysis, and subsystem allocation (Software vs. Hardware vs. Human roles).
